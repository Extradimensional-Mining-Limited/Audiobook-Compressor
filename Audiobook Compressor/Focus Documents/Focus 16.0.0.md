Subject: Request for Proposal: MainViewModel Modularization Strategy  
Greetings Vanguard,

My name is Telos. I have assumed the role of Strategist, taking over from Axion who has completed his operational arc. I look forward to our collaboration.

**1\. Context**

As the architect of the recent, successful MVVM refactor, you have a uniquely detailed understanding of the MainViewModel's current state.

Building on that success, the next strategic priority is to continue the modularization of the codebase.

**2\. The Architect's Intent**

The guiding principle for this task is to "modularize the MainViewModel as much as makes sense, without turning a virtue into a vice." The goal is not modularization for its own sake, but the pursuit of an elegant, maintainable, and robust architecture.

**3\. Request for Proposal**

We request that you leverage your recent experience to propose a strategy for this modularization.

Your proposal should include:

* **Codebase Overview:** To facilitate my onboarding, please provide a high-level architectural overview. This should include a map of the primary components (ViewModels, Services, Models) and a brief description of their current responsibilities and interactions.  
* **Candidate Identification:** An identification of the specific "code clusters" (e.g., validation, settings management, file helpers, etc.) that are prime candidates for being refactored into their own services.  
* **Core Responsibilities:** A clear definition of the logic and responsibilities that you believe *should remain* within the MainViewModel.  
* **Proposed Roadmap:** A recommended, high-level sequence for tackling this refactoring. Which module or service should be extracted first and why?  
* **Potential Pitfalls:** Any risks or complexities you foresee in this process.

This proposal will serve as the foundation for the Focus documents that will guide the Implementer.
