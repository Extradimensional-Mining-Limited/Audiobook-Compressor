## **Subject: Historical Directives**

This folder contains a historical archive of Focus.md directives that were used to guide the development of the Audiobook Compressor project.

These documents are preserved as a record of our decision-making process. They were often addressed to specific AI instances, such as "Sir Fixalot," who were active on the project at the time.

While they provide valuable insight into the project's evolution, the definitive, up-to-date rules and procedures for all current and future AI collaborators are located in the main Documentation folder, specifically in the **AI-Collaboration-SOP.md** file.
