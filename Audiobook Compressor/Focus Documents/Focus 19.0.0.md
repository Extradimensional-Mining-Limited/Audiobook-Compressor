Welcome to the Audiobook Compressor project. We are pleased to have you onboard as a Consultant. Your first task is a foundational one: to conduct a deep and contemplative review of the entire codebase.

**1\. Objective: Deep Contemplation and Strategic Review**

You are to immerse yourself in the codebase and our project documentation. We are not looking for a simple bug hunt, but for a deep, synthesized analysis. As you review, keep the following points in mind:

* **The Resistant Gremlins:** Pay special attention to the history of bug \#33 (the advanced panel visibility issue). What is your assessment of its resistant nature?  
* **General Issues:** What other points of friction, in-elegance, or potential bugs do you observe?  
* **Options for Improvement:** What opportunities do you see for improving the existing architecture, performance, or user experience?  
* **Refactoring for Elegance:** How could the code be refactored for greater elegance? We define elegance as "the smallest code with the greatest conceptual simplicity." However, do not make a vice of a virtue; a slightly larger solution that is significantly more powerful or clearer is often preferable.

**2\. Deliverables**

Your analysis is to be delivered as a direct update to two key documents in our "external brain":

1. **Review.md:** You are to reshape this document to reflect your own thinking. It should become a comprehensive summary of your findings, organized in a way that you believe most effectively communicates the state of the codebase.  
2. **ToDo.md:** Based on your review, you are to update the ToDo.md list. This may involve adding new tasks, re-prioritizing existing ones, or suggesting changes to the structure of the upcoming development cycles.

**3\. Reporting**

Upon completion of your review and the updates to the documents, please submit a single executive summary that outlines the key findings of your analysis and the most significant changes you have made to the Review.md and ToDo.md files.
